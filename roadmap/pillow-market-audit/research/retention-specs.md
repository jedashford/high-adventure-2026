**Question:** Marketing says the stickers 'adhere to most camping mats (not just Sea to Summit's)' but support reportedly says S2S mats only. Which is it, and will they hold on a Big Agnes pad?

FAILURE, and it is exactly Jed's failure — 'I had to super glue on the velcro after the first night, as I woke up and the pillow had moved and the velcro was still stuck on the pillow.' The patch released from the PAD, in ONE night, for a mover.
