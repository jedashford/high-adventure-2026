## Caveats worth surfacing in the UI

- Six of these products are **delisted at REI** (product URL 301s to a category page), so REI
  ratings are permanently unavailable for them: Sea to Summit Frontier UL Long Handle Spoon (231324),
  humangear GoBites Uno (131279), GSI Halulite Boiler 1.8 L (107822), GSI Infinity Bowl (895461),
  MSR PanHandler (114923), Light My Fire Spork (784705).
- Amazon numbers are **ratings**, not written reviews, and Amazon aggregates across a variation
  family (color/size/pack). Where the UI copy says "(N reviews)", the Amazon-sourced rows are
  technically "(N ratings)".
- Small-n rows to treat with care in a sort-by-rating: TOAKS 450 ml cup (9), Vargo ULV (9),
  TOAKS POT-1300 (4), Sea to Summit spoon (11), Wilderness Wash Pocket Soap (11), MSR PanHandler (15).
