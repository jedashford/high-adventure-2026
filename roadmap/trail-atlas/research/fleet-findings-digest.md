Confidence is high on the big picture: Toxaway and Alice have the most group-capable sites, Twin Lakes is the quieter first/last-night choice, Farley is limited and fills Friday nights, Edith is effectively closed to groups, and no campfires are legal at any in-wilderness lake on the route. Tent-count numbers are largely undocumented in sources and are marked unverified rather than estimated; camp-area coordinates other than GNIS lake centers are labeled approximate.
