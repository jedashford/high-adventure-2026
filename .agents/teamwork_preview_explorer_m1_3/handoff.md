### 1.2 Identified Problem & Ergonomic Target
While foam pads (like the Z Lite Sol) are cheap ($45) and puncture-proof, youth side-sleepers (and cold sleepers at 8,500 ft in August Sawtooth mountain nights dropping into 30°F) require **plush side-sleeper cushioning (>= 3.0 inches)** and **extra width (~25 inches)** so hips/shoulders do not bottom out on hard granite and arms do not slip off onto frozen dirt.

### 1.3 Target Criteria Matrix
- **Height Fit**: 5'1" – 5'4" (~61" – 64" torso/body length, accommodated by 60"–66" Short-Wide or 72" Regular-Wide pads).
- **Thickness**: `>= 3.0 inches` (eliminates hip/shoulder pressure points for side-sleepers).
- **Width**: `Wide ~25 inches` (prevents arms/elbows from dropping onto cold tent floor).
- **Weight**: Under `1.5 lbs (24 oz)`, ideal range `14 – 21 oz` (keeps pad within <7% of total 20-22 lb youth pack limit).
- **Budget**: Reasonably priced value range `$70 – $150`.
- **Thermal Rating**: `R-Value >= 3.0` (ASTM F3340-18 verified warmth for cold 35°F nights).

## 2. Logic Chain

### 2.1 Physiological & Ergonomic Rationale
1. **Thickness (>= 3.0")**: A youth weighing 80–110 lbs sleeping on their side concentrates body mass at the hip bone and shoulder. On a 1.5"–2.0" pad, hips press through to the cold ground. A 3.0"–3.5" air chamber creates a low-pressure air cushion allowing natural spine alignment.
2. **Width (~25" Wide)**: Standard 20" narrow pads force arms to rest outside the insulated pad area. A 25" wide pad maintains continuous draft protection around the sleeping bag.
3. **Weight (< 24 oz)**: Youth pack weight ceiling is 20–22 lbs (25% of body weight). A 24 oz pad ceiling ensures lightweight backpacking capability without dragging down endurance.
4. **Length Sizing (Short-Wide 60"–66" vs Regular-Wide 72")**: A 5'1"–5'4" youth fits on a 60" (5'0") or 66" (5'6") Short-Wide pad with head on pillow, saving 3–5 oz of packed bulk compared to adult Long pads.

## 3. Caveats

1. **Weight Boundary for Klymit Static V Wide**: At 25 oz (1.56 lbs), the Klymit Insulated Static V Wide exceeds the strict 24 oz target by 1 oz. However, it is retained as the #1 budget wide option ($80) because no other pad offers 3.0" thickness and 25" width under $90.
2. **MSRP of Therm-a-Rest XLite NXT RW**: The NeoAir XLite NXT Regular Wide has an MSRP of $210, exceeding the $150 budget range. It is included as an aspirational ultralight benchmark (16 oz, R4.5) for families looking for top-tier gear or buying during 20-25% off member sales.
3. **Short-Wide Availability**: Sizing like 60" x 25" (Short-Wide) in Big Agnes Rapide SL or Exped Ultra sell out quickly during summer peak season. Standard 72" x 25" (Regular-Wide) is a universally available alternative that kids will not outgrow.
4. **Foam vs Air Trade-off**: Foam pads (Z Lite Sol) are indestructible (0% leak risk), but only 0.75" thick. They serve as excellent secondary pads or back-sleeper budget options.
