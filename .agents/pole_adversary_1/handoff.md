  adjustment, so it can't grow with 12–17yo scouts, can't be tuned for steep up/down,
  and can't pitch trekking-pole shelters at a set height. Briefing's core need
  (adjustability for growing kids + shelters) rules it out. Switchback tested and

  options." No 2026 roundup puts it in a general ultralight slot.
- **Replacement: Gossamer Gear LT5** — $195, **9.8oz/278g PER PAIR** (139g/4.9oz per
  pole), 3-piece telescoping **twist lock, fully adjustable 60–130cm**, collapses 60cm,
  EVA foam. Switchback "Best Ultralight Telescoping," CleverHiker + Treeline "Best
  Ultralight" 2026. Adjustable, shelter-compatible, lighter/pair than the Leki, cheaper.
  https://www.gossamergear.com/products/lt5-three-piece-carbon-trekking-poles-pair
