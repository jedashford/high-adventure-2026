The Loop in Three Dimensions

Live navigation over real terrain — and it works like a video: tap the map to pause, tap again to play. While it flies, drag to spin and tilt around the hiker (it keeps going — ⟲ Recenter swings back behind them) and scroll, pinch, or +/− to zoom. The ⏩ slider runs from a slow crawl to 8×, the ∠ slider tips the view from straight-down to skimming the horizon, and 🧭 Trail-up / 🔒 View locked chooses whether the screen turns with the switchbacks or the arrow does. Drag the elevation profile to jump to any mile.
